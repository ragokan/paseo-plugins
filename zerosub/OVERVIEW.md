Runs several Claude and ChatGPT (Codex) subscriptions side by side on one daemon, without signing in and out of the CLIs. Each agent uses one account, shown on an account button in its composer, and moves to another account when its own runs out.

- **Accounts:** open Accounts (ZeroSub) in the sidebar and add an account under Claude or ChatGPT. Sign-in works from any device; away from the host's own browser, it uses a code. Your existing CLI logins are listed as they are, and nothing changes until you add a second account. Each card shows the account's 5-hour and weekly usage and when they reset.
- **Per agent:** the account button, `/account <name>` or `/account default` moves one agent. Make default sends new agents, and open agents that follow the default, to that account. Disable an account for a while and its agents move to your other accounts.
- **Limits:** when Claude Code or Codex reports a session or weekly limit, the agent moves to the account with the most room left and carries on, and a short note in its timeline says so. New agents skip spent accounts until they reset. One agent changes accounts at most 4 times in 10 minutes. On Paseo 0.13 and later, ZeroSub reads the finished turn from the agent's timeline to find Claude's limit notice.
- **Usage display:** every account shows in Paseo's usage display under Claude · ZeroSub and ChatGPT · ZeroSub, with ZeroSub's last reading, so you can pin the ones you want. With several hosts, list them in order under Usage across hosts (e.g. `ragdevbox, ragmini, ragair`). A host then reuses a recent reading from the hosts before it, skipping any it can't reach, instead of asking the provider itself.
- **Claude and ChatGPT differ:** a Claude conversation moves to another account in place. A ChatGPT thread can't, since Codex encrypts its reasoning per account, so the task carries on in a continuation tab for the other account, reused on later switches. Optionally, when every account of one provider is out, a stopped chat can carry on in a new agent on the other provider.

Needs the `claude` and/or `codex` CLI on the daemon's machine, the same ones Paseo uses. Added accounts get their own CLI homes under `~/.paseo/zerosub`, sharing your settings, skills, plugins, MCP servers and conversation history. Claude usage comes from Anthropic's usage endpoint with each account's own sign-in, as Paseo's usage display reads it; Codex usage comes from `codex app-server`. Credentials stay in the CLIs' own stores. Switching an open agent reloads it with the `paseo` CLI. On a password-protected daemon that reload can't connect, so the agent switches the next time its session starts.

Based on ZeroSub 1.3.0 by Kapybara (MIT), with its open Paseo 0.13 fix, a fix that lets the account button move an agent still on a spent account, and shared usage readings.
