Runs several Claude subscriptions side by side on one daemon, beside your ChatGPT (Codex) CLI login, without signing in and out of the CLIs. Each agent uses one account and moves to another when its own runs out.

- **Accounts:** open Accounts (ZeroSub) in the sidebar and add an account under Claude. Sign-in works from any device; away from the host's own browser, it uses a code. Your existing CLI logins are listed as they are, and nothing changes until you add a second account. Each card shows the account's 5-hour and weekly usage and when they reset.
- **Per agent:** `/account <name>` or `/account default` moves one agent. Make default sends new agents, and open agents that follow the default, to that account, including ones spread across accounts or moved off a limit; an agent you picked an account for stays. Disable an account for a while and its agents move to your other accounts.
- **Limits:** when Claude Code reports a session or weekly limit, or a usage reading shows an account full, its agents move to the accounts with the most room left, spread out rather than all on one, and carry on, and a short note in each timeline says so. New agents skip spent accounts until they reset. One agent changes accounts at most 4 times in 10 minutes. On Paseo 0.13 and later, ZeroSub reads the finished turn from the agent's timeline to find Claude's limit notice.
- **Usage display:** every account shows in Paseo's usage display under Claude · ZeroSub and ChatGPT · ZeroSub, with ZeroSub's last reading, so you can pin the ones you want. With several hosts, list them in order under Usage across hosts (e.g. `ragdevbox, ragmini, ragair`). A host then reuses a recent reading from the hosts before it, skipping any it can't reach, instead of asking the provider itself.
- **ChatGPT:** Codex encrypts each thread's reasoning per ChatGPT account, so a thread can't move to another one, and ZeroSub keeps Codex on your CLI login. It shows that login's usage and notes in the timeline when a limit frees up.

Needs the `claude` and/or `codex` CLI on the daemon's machine, the same ones Paseo uses. Added Claude accounts get their own CLI homes under `~/.paseo/zerosub`, sharing your settings, skills, plugins, MCP servers and conversation history. Claude usage comes from Anthropic's usage endpoint with each account's own sign-in, as Paseo's usage display reads it; Codex usage comes from `codex app-server`. Credentials stay in the CLIs' own stores. Switching an open agent reloads it with the `paseo` CLI. On a password-protected daemon that reload can't connect, so the agent switches the next time its session starts.

Based on ZeroSub 1.3.0 by Kapybara (MIT), with its open Paseo 0.13 fix, shared usage readings and routing fixes, and without several ChatGPT accounts, forks to the other provider or the composer account button.
